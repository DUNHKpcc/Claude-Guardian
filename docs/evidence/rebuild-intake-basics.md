### T02 clauses

1. **SKILL “开始方式”, instruction 2:** reuse supplied facts before asking gaps. The first missing business question is conditional on being missing; a fully supplied summary must not be forced through the opening again.
2. **Intake “Scheduling”, first paragraph, sentences 1–3:** read existing task facts, ask one decision per turn, absorb every useful fact in a reply and skip known/not-applicable items. This is the exact host-to-`record_facts` contract tested by one complete batch.
3. **Intake same paragraph, clarification sentence:** one focused clarification is followed by unknown/refusal/pause/limited-plan exits. A genuine gap can be queried without becoming repeated basic questioning or endless confirmation.
4. **Intake multi-fact/local-environment task paragraph:** one turn can absorb journey, current task, OS and route, while subscription evidence/source/time are preserved. Task labels are not permission or fabricated payment history. The candidate verifies those distinctions rather than equating “expert” with “paid”.
5. **Intake “Fact states and gates”, fact-envelope sentences:** value, state, source, target and observation time remain together; unknown/refused are not false and protected facts cannot be inferred. A supplied summary is still reported unless separately observed under an authorized mechanism.
6. **Intake G1 sentence:** coverage accounts for every applicable branch and visible unknowns. A complete summary can therefore finish without re-asking known questions, while an actual omitted prerequisite still remains a gap.
7. **Intake G2 sentence:** personalization needs enough current target/task/route/protection and relevant network/payment/resource facts. The test's “complete” refers to that chosen task, not universal native validation.
8. **Intake G3 sentence:** exact change/purchase, current prerequisites, scope/price, specific authorization, supported capability and recovery are still required. Approval cannot manufacture execution capability, and summary completeness cannot replace approval.
9. **Contact policy “Permissions and reporting”, first paragraph:** question answers, plan acceptance, host permission, OS privilege, first contact and payment are distinct. Silence/defaults/elapsed time grant none of the others. These sentences prevent the expert fast path from weakening action-time safeguards.
10. **Contact policy authorized-read paragraph:** actual reads still bind target, minimum fields, timeout and redaction; denied or unavailable capability does not authorize elevation. The tests' no-I/O sentinels exercise the pure intake boundary, not a native read approval bypass.
11. **SKILL independent-repair paragraph:** the Agent may investigate and propose scoped repair, but cannot provide its own True callback instead of user confirmation. This is consistent with blocked G3 and does not require unnecessary approval for ordinary explanations.
12. **SKILL protection/persistence sentences and intake persistence API contract:** save/export requires explicit scoped consent and no public state dump. An expert summary does not confer permission to save private facts merely because they are complete.
